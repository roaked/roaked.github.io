## **Behind the Scenes**

As a Lead Data Scientist, I design and architect scalable AI systems, transforming business concepts into production-ready solutions. My work lies at the intersection between applied data science and research, managing the entire ML lifecycle from exploratory data analysis to deployed model evaluation. I am particularly interested in uncertainty quantification using probabilistic and Bayesian methods to enhance data-driven decisions within engineering domains.

I was previously associated with the [Center of Intelligent Systems](https://csi.idmec.tecnico.ulisboa.pt/) at the [Technical University of Lisbon](https://tecnico.ulisboa.pt/en/) and contributed to research of the [Institute of Materials Science](https://www.tugraz.at/institute/imat/home) at the [Technical University of Graz](https://www.tugraz.at/en/home). 

![1223e](https://live.staticflickr.com/65535/53729234907_9e1594ce05_c.jpg)

Since how we collaborate in a team matters so much, here is a bit about how I like to work. As a natural planner, I organize both work and personal trips up to a year in advance. On a daily basis, I like to protect my early morning hours for focused sprints on complex tasks, which is when I am most productive. To balance this deep work with leadership responsibilities, I keep weekly calendar buffers for ad-hoc tasks and team support. For alignment, I rely on virtual whiteboards to simplify functional requirements for both colleagues and business stakeholders.


Outside of work, a flexible schedule lets me spend plenty of time in the mountains. I ski tour in the winter (averaging 25 days on snow) and shift to climbing high alpine peaks and sky running over 30km in the summer. Between mountain seasons, I also run road races, having finished more than 20 city half marathons across different countries over the last five years.
